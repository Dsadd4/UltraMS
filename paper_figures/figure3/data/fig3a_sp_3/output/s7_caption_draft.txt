Supplementary Figure S7 | Global and peak views assemble a molecular decision. NPLIB1 spectrum-to-molecule retrieval used 3,699 held-out ms2peaks_min50 fold-query evaluations from 3,352 unique spectra and three molecule-disjoint folds. (a) Paired target-rank paths from each UltraMS source view to the same validation-selected final fusion. The sources are shown independently, not as sequential computation stages. Folds 1 and 3 used equal global and raw-projection weights; fold 2 used equal global and projected-peak weights. (b) Exact source-to-final top-1 transitions. UltraMS global-to-final rescued 187, preserved 1033 and harmed 67 queries (net +3.24 percentage points); DreaMS global-to-fusion rescued 160, preserved 873 and harmed 93 queries (net +1.81 percentage points). Error bars are 95% target-molecule cluster-bootstrap intervals. (c) Net top-1 change across strata defined without model outputs. (d) Global-plus-peak replication on 16,440 MassSpecGym test queries per candidate task; raw projection was not part of this protocol. (e) Deterministic median-transition cases from the shared hard subset of queries with 50 candidates and 150 retained peaks. Cases illustrate the population-defined outcome lanes and are not independent statistical evidence.
